Privacy Policy

Meet Notetaker Recorder

Provided by Boosta · Last updated 17 July 2026 · Contact: vladyslav.strelbytskyi@boosta.com

Meet Notetaker Recorder is an internal tool used within Boosta to record Google Meet meetings and turn them into transcripts and summaries. This policy explains what data the extension handles, why, and who it is shared with.

What data we process

  • Meeting recordings — when you start a recording, the extension captures the audio and video of the active Google Meet tab together with your microphone. Recordings contain the content of the meeting, including spoken words and anything shared on screen.
  • Your account email — obtained through Google sign-in (the userinfo.email scope) to identify who uploaded a recording.
  • Names of meeting participants — while a recording is running, the extension reads the display names shown in the Google Meet participant list. This is done so that the transcript and the summary can name the people who took part instead of labelling them “Speaker 1”, “Speaker 2”. These names are stored alongside the recording and are used only to produce the meeting notes. No participant names are read when you are not recording.
  • Speaker activity timing — while a recording is running, the extension also notes which participant Google Meet is showing as currently speaking, and stores those moments as timestamps alongside the recording. This is used solely to attribute transcript lines to the right participant. The extension performs no audio analysis or voice profiling, and nothing is observed when you are not recording.
  • Your settings — the destination Google Drive folder and Slack channel you choose, stored locally in your browser.

How we use it

Recordings are uploaded to Google Drive and processed by Boosta's own pipeline to produce a transcript and a meeting summary, which are delivered to the relevant team (for example, via Slack). Transcription and summarization are performed using third-party AI processing providers (currently OpenRouter and Google Gemini) acting as service providers on Boosta's behalf. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it for any purpose unrelated to producing meeting notes.

Storage and retention

Recordings are stored in Google Drive. The raw video file is automatically deleted after 30 days; the transcript and summary are retained as meeting history so the team can refer back to them.

Who it is shared with

Your data is accessible to Boosta and to the service providers used to deliver this functionality — Google (Drive storage and Google Meet) and the AI transcription/summarization providers named above — strictly to provide the recording and summary features. It is not sold or shared with any other third parties.

Your rights and contact

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, or ask any privacy question, by contacting vladyslav.strelbytskyi@boosta.com. As this is an internal tool operated within the boosta.com Google Workspace, Boosta is the controller of the data described above.

This extension is distributed privately for internal use at Boosta and is not offered to the general public.
